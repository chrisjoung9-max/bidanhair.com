About BIDAN

An American hardware company for hair, founded in 2026.

Engineering

We are building one product for hair, and building our own test rigs to measure it before anything is decided. The product follows from those measurements.

What we say about it will come with the measurement behind it.

Materials

Materials are chosen for how they behave over years, not how they look in a photograph. That is slower, and it is the only way to build something worth keeping.

Testing

We build our own test rigs and measure before we decide. Design, engineering and testing are done by the same small team, which keeps the distance between an idea and a measurement short.

StandardsSafety, durability and repairability are design requirements from the first sketch, not certifications added at the end.
