Part-time test engineer, contract

You can commit to fixed weekday blocks every week, and you have US work authorization or OPT.

Pay is $2,000 to $2,500 a month as a contractor, depending on experience. All of the work happens at Hangar6 on company equipment, so it stays fully separate from university research.
